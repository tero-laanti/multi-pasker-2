Recording booth

Every voice line in MULTI-PASKER 2. Choose the game's assets/voice folder once, then for each card: Record, say the line, Stop. The take is trimmed, evened out and saved there as WAV under the card's file name, and plays back so you can hear it. Record a card again to replace it. Keys: Space record / stop, ↑ ↓ pick a card, Enter play the take, P play the machine-voice example.

Until you choose it, takes download as files instead.
